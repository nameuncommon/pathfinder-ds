## Design Context

Drawn from the approved specs (vault `_projects/_pathfinder/pathfinderSystemSpec.html`, `pathfinderAppSpec.html`, `pathfinderDsStorybookV2Spec.html`). Fictional brand, portfolio piece; no Stampin' Up! content.

### Users
People who plan on paper and want the paper to stay the center: first Jake (Leuchtturm notebook, Notion, Claude), then anyone who plans on paper with no prior method. Two surfaces: an A5 paper insert that runs the week, and an app that organizes everything the paper touches. The phone is used for a two-minute morning ritual (snap the spread, check what it read, sort a few inbox items) and for capture on the move; the desktop is for planning ahead on Sunday. Storybook's audience is a design-system reviewer (hiring manager, design lead) who should find a complete, documented, tested library that matches Figma.

### Brand Personality
Calm, encouraging, deliberate. A good companion that keeps notes: it cheers you on and never scolds, asks before it decides, and lets dropping be a decision rather than a failure. Wayfinding is a hint (the compass sort, bearings, destinations), never a costume. Emotional goals: calm at 7am, a small lift from "6 done yesterday", confidence that nothing is lost.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark navy (navy/950) field, gold as the single accent, cream as paper and text, frosted glass surfaces with a solid fallback, a faint dot field and two capped soft glows. Type: Cormorant Garamond SemiBold for titles (the brand's voice), Geist for UI, Geist Mono for data. Logo: the dial (ring around the compass rose P). Dark-only for now; light is a later pass. Anti-references: a game (streaks, points, red overdue badges), an everything-app with no backbone, a generic to-do app in a wayfinding costume, AI-slop dashboards.

### Design Principles
1. Cream means paper: cream surfaces hold only things that came from the book.
2. It cheers you on, it never scolds; it asks, you decide.
3. Every screen has one job and one primary action in thumb reach.
4. Contrast is measured, not assumed (worst case: glass over the 10% gold glow); never colour alone.
5. Stay true to what is designed: tokens.json is the single source, Figma and code mirror it.
